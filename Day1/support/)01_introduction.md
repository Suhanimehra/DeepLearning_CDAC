## What is Deep Learning?
- Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that focuses on neural networks with many layers (deep neural networks) to model complex patterns in data. 
- It is particularly effective for tasks such as image recognition, natural language processing, and speech recognition.

### Parts of Deep Learning
1. Artificial Neural Networks (ANN)
2. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN)
3. Recurrent Neural Networks (RNN)
4. Long Short-Term Memory Networks (LSTM)
5. Transformers

1.  What is an Artificial Neural Network?
-   Computational model that emulates the way biological neural networks in the human brain 
operate.
- It consists of interconnected nodes or "neurons" that process and transmit information.
- ANNs are designed to recognize patterns, learn from data, and make predictions or decisions 
without being explicitly programmed for a specific task. 

### History:
 - 1943: Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts : artificial neurons
 - 1958: Frank Rosenblatt introduced the perceptron : neural network with the ability to learn
 - 1969: Marvin Minsky and Seymour Papert’s : highlighted limitations of the perceptron
 - 1980s: The backpropagation algorithm : popular method for training neural networks.
 - Late 2000s:  Advent of powerful GPUs and large datasets, 
 - Deep Learning, a subset of machine learning focusing on neural networks with many layers, began to gain prominence.

 
### ANN with modern computing
- leverages powerful GPUs and large datasets to train deep neural networks efficiently, enabling breakthroughs in various AI applications.
- Availability of large amounts of data, which neural networks require for effective training.
- Increase in computational power, especially with the advent of GPUs. 
- The realisation that deeper neural networks (i.e., those with more hidden layers) could achieve remarkable results on complex tasks.

### Breakthrough
- CNN - Convolutional Neural Networks are specialized for processing grid-like data such as images, using convolutional layers to automatically learn spatial hierarchies of features.
- RNN - Recurrent Neural Networks are designed for sequential data, where connections between nodes form directed cycles, allowing information to persist across time steps.
- LSTM - Long Short-Term Memory Networks are a type of RNN that can capture long-term dependencies in sequences, addressing the vanishing gradient problem.
- Transformers - A neural network architecture that relies on self-attention mechanisms to process sequences in parallel, achieving state-of-the-art results in NLP and other tasks.

### Real-World Applications of ANN
- Image recognition : Identifying objects, people, or scenes in images.
- speech recognition : Converting spoken language into text.
- Natural language processing : Understanding and generating human language.
- Autonomous driving : Enabling self-driving cars to perceive and navigate the environment.
- Medical diagnosis : Assisting doctors in detecting and diagnosing diseases.
- Financial forecasting : Predicting market trends and financial outcomes.

### Benefits of ANN
- **Adaptability** : ANNs can adjust to new data and changing environments.
- **Pattern Recognition**: Their ability to identify intricate patterns makes them suitable for tasks like image and speech recognition.
- **Tolerance to Noisy Data**: ANNs can handle and make accurate predictions even when the input data contains noise or errors.
- **Parallel Processing**: ANNs can perform multiple computations simultaneously, leading to faster processing and efficiency.

### Industry Impact of ANN
- Healthcare : Assisting in medical diagnosis and treatment planning.
- Finance : Enhancing fraud detection and financial forecasting.
- Automotive : Enabling autonomous driving and advanced driver-assistance systems.
- Retail : Improving customer recommendations and inventory management.
- Manufacturing : Optimizing production processes and predictive maintenance.
